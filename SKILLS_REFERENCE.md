# Hero Skills - Quick Reference Guide

## 🗡️ MELEE SKILLS

### Power Strike 💥
**Trigger:** Charge → Smash (Heavy Attack) [First attack only]
**Effect:** 
- Base: 6 (Smash) + 2 (Charge) = 8 damage
- With Skill: 8 + 3 = **11 damage**
- Ignores Block defense
- 50% chance to Stun target
**Use Case:** Burst damage to break through defenses
**Counters:** Evade, Counter (but takes full damage)

---

### Blade Fury ⚔️
**Trigger:** Ready → Strike (Normal Attack) x2
**Effect:**
- Each attack hits TWICE
- Each hit deals 2 damage
- Total: 2 attacks × 2 hits × 2 damage = **8 damage**
**Use Case:** Consistent multi-hit damage
**Counters:** Each hit can be blocked separately

---

### Counter Stance 🛡️
**Trigger:** Have both Block + Counter in hand, play in sequence
**Effect:**
- Next attack taken: reduces damage by 5 AND reflects 100%
- Example: 6 damage attack → take 1, reflect 6
**Use Case:** Defensive turn with devastating counter
**Duration:** Until next attack received

---

## 🏹 RANGED SKILLS

### Headshot 🎯
**Trigger:** Focus → Focus Shot (Charge Shot) [First attack only]
**Effect:**
- Base: 6 (Focus Shot) + 1 (Focus) = 7 damage
- With Skill: 7 × 1.5 = **10.5 damage (rounded to 10)**
- Cannot be Blocked
- Cannot be Evaded
- Ignores Shield
**Use Case:** Guaranteed kill on low HP targets
**Counters:** Counter/Deflect (only options)

---

### Rapid Fire 🔫
**Trigger:** Quick Shot x3 in hand
**Effect:**
- Hits ALL enemy heroes
- 2 damage to each
- Total: 3 heroes × 2 = **6 damage distributed**
**Use Case:** Chip damage entire enemy team
**Counters:** Each hero can defend individually

---

### Sniper Focus 🔭
**Trigger:** Ready → Skip first attack → Use second attack
**Effect:**
- +4 damage bonus
- Cannot be Evaded
- Cannot be Blocked
**Use Case:** Patient setup for guaranteed hit

---

## 🔮 MAGE SKILLS

### Elemental Mastery 🌟
**Trigger:** Any Elemental Magic (Fire/Ice/Wind/Earth/Ivy/Lightning) → Blast (Heavy Magic)
**Effect:**
- Status duration: +2 turns
- Status DOT damage: +2
- Splash damage: 2 to adjacent heroes
**Examples:**
- Fire: Burn lasts 4 turns (2+2), deals 4/turn (2+2)
- Ice: Freeze lasts 4 turns
- Poison: Lasts 5 turns (3+2), deals 5/turn (3+2)
**Use Case:** Maximum status effect destruction
**Counters:** Cure card, Deflect (reflects status to you!)

---

### Arcane Surge ✨
**Trigger:** Bolt (Normal Magic) x2 consecutively
**Effect:**
- Each attack: +4 damage
- Ignores Shield defense
- 50% chance to chain to random enemy
**Damage Breakdown:**
- Primary targets: 2 × (3 + 4) = 14 damage
- Chain targets: potentially 2 × 3.5 = 7 bonus damage
**Use Case:** High burst magic damage
**Counters:** Shield defense doesn't work, use Deflect

---

### Spell Reflect 🔮
**Trigger:** [REACTIVE] When receiving magic attack + play Deflect
**Effect:**
- Reflects 100% damage (instead of 50%)
- Reflects status effect back to attacker
- Reflected damage gets +2 bonus
**Example:**
- Enemy casts Fire + Blast (6 damage + Burn)
- You reflect: 6 + 2 = 8 damage to them + they get Burned
**Use Case:** Ultimate magic defense
**Counters:** Physical/Ranged attacks don't trigger it

---

### Frost Nova ❄️
**Trigger:** Ice → Attack x2 consecutively
**Effect:**
- ALL enemies take 1 damage
- ALL enemies get Frozen (1 turn)
**Use Case:** Team crowd control
**Counters:** Cure removes Freeze

---

## 💚 SUPPORT SKILLS

### Divine Protection ✝️
**Trigger:** Shield x2 on different allies
**Effect:**
- ALL allies gain +5 shield
- ALL allies take -2 damage for 1 turn
**Use Case:** Team survival turn
**Duration:** Shield permanent, damage reduction 1 turn

---

### Mass Heal 💚
**Trigger:** Heal → Heal consecutively
**Effect:**
- ALL allies heal 4 HP
- Removes Burn, Poison, Bleed from all allies
**Total Healing:** 4 HP × 3 heroes = 12 HP team healing
**Use Case:** Team recovery from DOT effects
**Counters:** N/A (can't be prevented)

---

### Purification 🌟
**Trigger:** Cure → Shield on same target
**Effect:**
- Target: removes ALL status effects
- Target: immune to status for 1 turn
- Target: heals 3 HP
**Use Case:** Save ally from multiple status effects
**Duration:** Immunity lasts 1 turn

---

### Blessing 🙏
**Trigger:** Heal x3 on same target
**Effect:**
- Target deals +3 damage (2 turns)
- Target takes -3 damage (2 turns)
**Use Case:** Buff frontline hero for assault
**Duration:** 2 turns

---

## 🌟 UNIVERSAL SKILLS (Any Class)

### Desperate Strike 💀
**Trigger:** Hero HP ≤ 25% + Charge + Any Attack
**Effect:**
- 2x damage multiplier
- Self-damage: 5 HP
- Cannot be blocked or evaded
**Example:**
- Smash (6) + Charge (2) = 8
- With skill: 8 × 2 = **16 damage**
- You take 5 damage
**Use Case:** All-or-nothing finisher
**Risk:** Might defeat yourself!

---

### Last Stand ⚔️
**Trigger:** Last hero alive on your team + Block card
**Effect:**
- All incoming damage: -10
- Counter damage: 3 to all attackers
**Duration:** 1 turn
**Use Case:** Final desperate defense
**Example:** 
- Enemy hits for 6 damage
- You take: max(0, 6 - 10) = 0 damage
- Enemy takes: 3 counter damage

---

## 📊 SKILL TIER LIST

### S-Tier (Best Skills)
- **Power Strike**: Highest burst damage, penetration
- **Headshot**: Unavoidable damage, shield bypass
- **Elemental Mastery**: Strongest status effects
- **Mass Heal**: Team sustain + DOT removal

### A-Tier (Very Strong)
- **Arcane Surge**: High magic burst
- **Divine Protection**: Team defense
- **Desperate Strike**: Game-ending damage

### B-Tier (Situational but Good)
- **Frost Nova**: AoE crowd control
- **Blade Fury**: Consistent damage
- **Blessing**: Long-lasting buff

### C-Tier (Very Situational)
- **Last Stand**: Only when losing
- **Counter Stance**: Requires specific cards

## 💡 SKILL COMBOS & STRATEGIES

### Opening Combo (Turn 1-2)
**Ranged Hero:**
1. Turn 1: Play Focus
2. Turn 2: Play Focus Shot → **Headshot** → 10 unavoidable damage

### Status Lock Combo
**Mage Hero:**
1. Play Lightning Magic
2. Play Blast → **Elemental Mastery**
3. Enemy is Paralyzed for 3 turns (1+2) - can't use skills!
4. Follow up with more attacks while they're helpless

### Defensive Pivot
**Support Hero:**
1. Team is low HP with status effects
2. Play Heal → Heal → **Mass Heal**
3. All allies heal 4 HP + cleansed
4. Next turn: Shield x2 → **Divine Protection** → all allies +5 shield + damage reduction

### Execute Combo
**Melee Hero:**
1. Enemy hero at 11 HP
2. Play Charge
3. Play Smash → **Power Strike** → 11 damage
4. Exact lethal + possible stun on others

### Team Wipe Setup
**Ranged Hero:**
1. Draw 3x Quick Shot
2. Play all three → **Rapid Fire**
3. All enemies take 2 damage
4. Great for finishing multiple low-HP targets

## 🚫 SKILL PREVENTION

### Paralyze Status Effect
**Source:** Lightning Magic
**Effect:** Prevents ALL skills
**Duration:** 1 turn (3 with Elemental Mastery)
**Strategy:** Use to shut down enemy skill combos

### How to Avoid Skills
1. **Kill before they execute:** Interrupt their combo
2. **Paralyze:** Lightning Magic prevents skills
3. **Disrupt hand:** Force them to use cards defensively
4. **Stun/Freeze:** Prevent their turn entirely

## 🎮 ADVANCED TACTICS

### Skill Baiting
1. Show you have Charge in hand
2. Enemy plays Block expecting Power Strike
3. You play different attack without Charge
4. Enemy wasted Block card

### Resource Management
- Don't always go for skills
- Sometimes basic combos are more efficient
- Save skill combos for critical moments

### Turn Planning
- Count cards needed for skills
- Plan 2-3 turns ahead
- Consider enemy's possible reactions

### Adaptation
- Enemy has Deflect? Don't use Elemental Mastery
- Enemy has Evade? Use Headshot (unavoidable)
- Enemy has Block? Use Power Strike (ignores block)
